Thnderbolts review

Thunderbolts is a 2025 American Superhero Movie. It's based on Marvel Comics featuring the team Thunderbolts. It's the 36th film in the Marvel Cinematic Universe(MCU) The film is directed by newcomer to the MCU Jake Schreier with an ensemble cast featuring Florence Pugh, Sebastian Stan, Wyatt Russel, Olga Kurlyenko, Lewis Pullman, Geraldine Viswanathan, Chris Bauer, Wendell Pierce, David Harbour, Hannah John-Kamen, and Julia Louis-Dreyfus. In the film, a group of antiheroes are caught in a deadly trap and forced to work together on a dangerous mission.
